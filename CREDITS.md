# Credits

## Code

The application code is MIT licensed — see [LICENSE](LICENSE).

The oceanographic models are transcriptions of published work:

- **UNESCO (1983)** — *Algorithms for computation of fundamental properties of seawater*,
  UNESCO Technical Papers in Marine Science 44. (EOS-80 equation of state.)
- **Mackenzie, K. V. (1981)** — Nine-term equation for sound speed in the oceans,
  *J. Acoust. Soc. Am.* 70(3), 807–812.
- **Saunders, P. M. (1981)** — Practical conversion of pressure to depth,
  *J. Phys. Oceanogr.* 11, 573–574.
- **Jerlov, N. G. (1976)** — *Marine Optics*, Elsevier. (Attenuation coefficients.)
